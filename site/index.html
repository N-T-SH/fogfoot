Greenlit

Launching soon in Bengaluru · Domlur and Koramangala first

Turn Bengaluru's footpaths green.

Greenlit is a free, community map of Bengaluru's footpaths. Just walk with your phone's camera on: problems are tagged for you, and every street you cover turns from orange to green.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • Open source
  • One e-mail when it opens

How it works

  1. Just walkOpen the app, start a walk and keep the camera on. No forms, no typing, no photos to pick. The camera view also keeps the road ahead in sight while you check your phone.
  2. It tags the problemsAs you go, the footpath is checked and problems are tagged to the exact stretch automatically: parked vehicles, shop spillover, broken or uneven slabs, open drains, garbage, low wires, no footpath at all.
  3. PlayEvery 10 m you walk turns from orange to green on a map shared with everyone, and your metres add up. Cover your ward, keep it green as the month rolls on, and see how your neighbourhood compares. Points, streaks and ward standings will join in.
  4. MapTogether we build a public, current picture of which footpaths are usable and clean, starting in Domlur and Koramangala.

Why

In June 2026 the Supreme Court of India recognised the right to walk on footpaths as part of our fundamental rights. Most of us know what the pavement looks like on the ground, but there is no shared, current picture of it.

What we promise

  • Free and not for profit. No ads, no selling data.
  • Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what it does.
  • Private by design: photos stay on your phone, and shared walks record only which stretch was covered and on which day.
  • Built for ordinary phones, including low-end Android and iPhone. No app store needed.
