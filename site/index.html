fogfoot

Which footpaths can you actually walk on?

fogfoot is a free, community map of Bengaluru's footpaths. Walk, watch your footpath turn green, and help show which pavements are usable and clean.

Try the early prototype (Domlur, Koramangala)

How it works

  1. WalkOpen the app and start a walk. Your phone stays in your hand; the camera view keeps the road ahead in sight.
  2. CoverStreets you walk turn from orange to green. Green means someone walked that stretch in the last month; orange means nobody has yet.
  3. MapTogether we build a public picture of which footpaths are usable and clean, starting in Domlur and Koramangala.

Why

In June 2026 the Supreme Court of India recognised the right to walk on footpaths as part of our fundamental rights. Most of us know what the pavement looks like on the ground, but there is no shared, current picture of it.

Read a summary of the judgment (CPR)

What we promise

  • Free and not for profit. No ads, no selling data.
  • Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what it does.
  • Private by design: photos stay on your phone, and shared walks record only which stretch was covered and on which day.
  • Built for ordinary phones, including low-end Android and iPhone. No app store needed.
